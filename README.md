# a-goose-among-sirens
Repo for A Goose Among Sirens.

A Goose Among Sirens will ideally be an easy jackbox party pack style game, just a silly social event where people get together and introduce each other to some new music through the context of the game.
Premise-wise, each player is given prompts which ask them for songs representing various scernarios. The players submit their songs and the other players have to guess who submitted it. There's more on the specific game rules in the docx. file.
Ideally we can use Spotify or Youtube music to have the songs actually play when they're compiled. I don't know how feasible this is, but we'll need some way to play the music or there's really no game.
